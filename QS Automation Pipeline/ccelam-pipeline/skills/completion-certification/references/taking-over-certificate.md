# Taking-Over Certificate — Structure and Clause Logic

FIDIC Red Book 1999 SC 10.1 (2017: SC 10.1, with the Engineer's 28-day
obligation retained). Adapt clause numbers to the contract edition recorded
in the ledger header.

## Document structure (.docx, A4 portrait, CCELAM brand)

1. **Certificate header**
   - Document ref: `CCELAM/TOC/[PROJECT]/[YEAR]/[SEQ] Rev [N]`
   - Project title, Employer, Contractor, Engineer, contract ref and form
   - Whether the certificate covers the Works, a Section, or a part (10.2)

2. **Recitals**
   - Contractor's application date and reference (start of the 28-day clock)
   - Inspection date(s), attendees, photographic record reference
   - Tests on Completion: certificates cited, results summarised (9.1)

3. **Certification clause** (the operative paragraph, passive voice)
   > "Pursuant to Sub-Clause 10.1 of the Conditions of Contract, it is
   > hereby certified that the Works [/ Section ...] were completed in
   > accordance with the Contract on [DATE], save for the minor outstanding
   > works and defects listed in the Schedule appended hereto, which do not
   > substantially affect the use of the Works for their intended purpose."

4. **Schedule of Outstanding Works and Defects** — appended, see
   `defects-schedule.md`; state the total value (from the rate library)
   and the completion target dates.

5. **Consequences recorded** (each with its Sub-Clause):
   - Defects Notification Period commences [date], expires [date] (1.1.3.7)
   - First moiety of retention released: **[amount from ledger event]** (14.9)
   - Care of the Works passes to the Employer (17.2)
   - Delay damages cease to accrue from the completion date (8.7)
   - Insurance arrangements transition (18.2)

6. **Sign-off block** — signature box, official stamp box, date field.

## Partial taking over (10.2)

- Retention release is proportionate to the value of the part taken over.
- Delay damages for the remainder reduce in proportion to the value
  certified as taken over (10.2 final paragraph).
- State the valuation basis (the ledger's measured value of the part).

## Traps

- **Deemed taking over**: silence for 28 days after a valid application =
  deemed certificate. Enter the deadline in the notice register the day
  the application arrives.
- Do not certify against an incomplete Tests on Completion record; if tests
  are prevented by the Employer, SC 10.3 applies instead.
- Use-and-occupation by the Employer before certification can trigger
  deemed taking over of the part used (10.2).
